## Why

Production Collection impressions record Collection positions and cosine scores. They cannot explain which readable member moved into an article slot or whether publication age changed that choice. An internal tester needs a same-request comparison while the proposed Discover selection is under evaluation.

## What Changes

An explicitly requested diagnostic for an authenticated internal account records the served member order and a server-side comparison of the proposed Discover selection in existing first-party telemetry. The reader continues to receive the ordinary recommendation response. The diagnostic uses the existing Collection shortlist, eligibility checks and stored representatives.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `collection-ranking-diagnostics` covers opt-in Collection and member rank comparisons.

## Impact

The API recommendation service, the internal email check and existing event producer are affected. No new database, event sink or client package is required.
